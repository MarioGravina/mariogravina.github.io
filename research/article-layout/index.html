RESEARCH TEMPLATE · UNPUBLISHED

Architecture, evidence, and the story behind a board

By Mario Gravina · Publication date to be assigned · Draft status

Formal articles will begin with a concise abstract explaining the research question, the hardware being examined, the evidence used, and the limits of what can reasonably be concluded.

1. Historical context

This section will situate the device within its technical and commercial era. Claims about model specifications, pricing, engineering decisions, and launch dates will be supported by references to period documentation whenever possible.

2. Technical evidence

Photographs, schematics, developer notes, and measurements can be presented as distinct forms of evidence. Observations and inferences should be labeled clearly rather than being combined into unsupported conclusions.

3. References and revisions

Completed articles will provide citations, photographic provenance, correction notes, and a visible record of material revisions.
